What is PDA?+

Preparation of Doses for Administration (PDA) is a pharmaceutical service that organizes orally administered solid medicines into personalized dispensing devices to improve treatment adherence and enhance the safety and effectiveness of a patient’s pharmacotherapy.

Flagship Product

Structure your PDA service with a reliable, compact, and scalable solution

Do your patients take several medications every day? The RDC 45 helps pharmacies make dose preparation safer, organize production more effectively, and provide a clear, personalized, and professional PDA service.

Guided preparation in 4 steps:

  1. 1

    Program the preparation

    The pharmacist enters the treatments, patients, and administration times into the software to set up production.

  2. 2

    Fill the cells

    Guided by the machine’s LEDs, the pharmacist loads the medicines into the indicated cells.

  3. 3

    Produce the dose sachets

    Once loading is complete, the RDC 45 automatically packages the medicines in printed, sealed sachets.

  4. 4

    Prepare delivery to the patient

    You check the finished sachets and place them in the MediMesk dispensing box before handing them to the patient.
